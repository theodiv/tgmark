# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This code of conduct sets the behavior expected of every participant in the
TGMark project, in every space the project manages.

## Expected Conduct

- Communicate with professionalism and respect, even during disagreements.
- Provide constructive, specific, and actionable feedback in code reviews.
- Respond to criticism on its technical merits.
- Credit prior work and the contributions of others.
- Address the contribution, not the contributor.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning or condescending language about a person or their work.
- Publishing private information, such as an email or physical address, without
  explicit consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy covers the project's GitHub repository (issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and commit messages) and anyone representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer interprets and enforces this code of conduct. Responses
to a violation are at the project maintainer's discretion and may include:

- A private warning naming the violation.
- Editing or removing the comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that
  violate this policy.
- Temporary or permanent exclusion from the project's spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately by email to `theodiv@gmail.com`. Reports are
kept confidential, and the project maintainer acknowledges each one.
